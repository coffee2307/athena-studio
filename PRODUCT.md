# Athena Studio — Product context

Athena Studio is a Vietnamese-language creative technology studio. Its public website helps a potential customer understand whether to commission a new website, maintain an existing website, or automate a workflow with AI.

## Visitors and jobs to be done

- Founders and small organizations who need a reliable website without writing a technical brief.
- Existing site owners who need maintenance or conversion improvements.
- Operators whose repeated tasks could benefit from AI integration.
- Mobile-first visitors who need to call, send a message, or ask for a scoped quote in a few taps.

## Primary success flows

1. Understand Athena's services within the first viewport.
2. Find a specific service and see scope and limits, not invented client results.
3. Inspect public portfolio screenshots without disclosing PHAM/ECONITH code or falsely calling founder work paid client work.
4. Reach Athena by functional email, phone, Zalo, or WhatsApp; desktop without a mail app must have usable web alternatives.
5. Sign up / log in to a real account *only after* identity backend, email verification, database, and permissions are configured. Local test accounts must be clearly labeled and cannot access production client projects.

## Positioning and voice

Premium editorial technology; capable, measured, customer-focused, entirely Vietnamese prose; URLs use English slugs. Do not expose internal implementation classifications, fake testimonials, unverifiable performance claims, or fake enabled features.

## Operational constraints

- Framework: Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript.
- No public source links to PHAM or ECONITH repositories.
- Preserve stable, accessibility-first mobile navigation and legible Vietnamese glyphs.
- No extra third-party runtime solely for visual flourishes.
- Release: test → GitHub PR/CI → merge to main → local preview.
